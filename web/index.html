The official built-in presets available in the Fairbuds app. “Studio” solves some issues of the “Main” preset (like sibilant loudness), but still noticeably lacks in treble.

A collection of presets generated with AutoEq based on frequency response curves from different sources. They use undocumented parts of the protocol to achieve greater accuracy.

It is unclear why these measurements vary so much. Possibly different firmware versions or fits. 🤷‍♂️

Due to different anatomy, no completely-universal neutral earbud target can exist. These presets should serve as a good base but in the end they are tuned to my personal preference; see this page for how to generate your own.

Custom (app-compatible)

Presets using only settings exposed by the official Fairbuds app. Use these if you want to play with the values, as they have a constant band width. You will not achieve as good a treble response though, as the EQ stops at 12 kHz and does not affect higher frequencies enough.

Why?

The Fairbuds are not tuned correctly.

Audio is mastered on speakers. When you plug audio directly into your ear canals, you bypass everything that would normally shape the sound, like the room, your head, torso and outer ear.

If you do not compensate for this, the sound will not sound natural to you. The Fairbuds are tuned more like speakers than earbuds.

Thankfully, the equaliser on them, albeit limited, is programmable, so we can use software like AutoEq to generate suitable presets.

This app includes some best-effort presets and handles the EQ control for you. Give it a try!
